# 🌙 SoLuna

### **Where Music, Intelligence & Expression Meet.**

**SoLuna** is an intelligent music platform that brings together **AI music generation, audio analysis, interactive instruments, music theory, and creative tools** into a single workspace.

From turning a simple thought into a musical composition to continuing an unfinished idea from a MIDI sample, SoLuna exists to make music feel less like a sequence of notes and more like something you can **discover, shape, and bring to life.**

> **“Where melodies find their moonlight, and imagination learns to sing.”** 🌙🎶

---

## 🎼 Generative Music

### ✨ Music from Prompts

Sometimes a song begins with nothing more than a feeling.

Describe the music you imagine in natural language, and SoLuna transforms that idea into a musical direction.

> *“A melancholic piano melody beneath a sky full of stars, slowly growing into an emotional cinematic finale.”*

Turn imagination into:

* 🎵 Melodies
* 🎹 Harmonies
* 🥁 Rhythms
* 🎻 Instrumentation
* 🌌 Atmosphere
* 💭 Emotion

**A thought becomes a prompt.**
**A prompt becomes music.**

---

### 🎹 Continue from MIDI

Every unfinished melody has somewhere it wants to go.

Give SoLuna a MIDI sample — a melody, chord progression, rhythm, or musical fragment — and let it explore what could come next.

SoLuna uses the existing musical context to generate a continuation while maintaining the character of the original idea.

```text
        Your Idea
           │
           ▼
      ┌───────────┐
      │ MIDI Input │
      └─────┬─────┘
            │
            ▼
   ┌──────────────────┐
   │ Musical Context  │
   │                  │
   │ Notes            │
   │ Rhythm           │
   │ Harmony          │
   │ Structure        │
   └────────┬─────────┘
            │
            ▼
      ┌────────────┐
      │  SoLuna AI │
      └──────┬─────┘
             │
             ▼
      ┌─────────────┐
      │ Continuation│
      └──────┬──────┘
             │
             ▼
       A New Chapter
```

> **You bring the beginning.**
> **SoLuna wonders what comes next.**

## 🤖 Intelligent Audio

### 🎼 Chord Analysis

Analyze musical audio and uncover the harmonic language hidden beneath the sound.

### 🎚️ Source Separation

Separate recordings into vocals, drums, bass, and other musical components using deep-learning-based source separation.

### 🎤 Vocal Extraction

Extract vocals to create isolated vocal tracks, instrumentals, or material for further experimentation.

### 🎛️ Precision Tuner

Accurate chromatic tuning for instruments and vocals.

---

## 🎮 Interactive Music Learning

### 🧠 Ear Training

Train your musical ear through interactive exercises covering:

* Interval recognition
* Chord identification
* Pitch recognition
* Progressive difficulty
* Streak-based practice

### 🎹 Scale Explorer

Explore scales and modes through their notes, intervals, chord relationships, and harmonic context.

### 🎸 Virtual Fretboard

Explore notes, intervals, scales, chords, and positions across an interactive guitar fretboard.

### 🎹 Virtual Piano

Play, experiment, and discover chords, scales, and melodies through an interactive 88-key piano.

# 🧠 The SoLuna Philosophy

Music rarely begins as music.

Sometimes it begins as a **feeling**.

Sometimes as a melody you hum at 2 AM.

Sometimes as four notes scribbled into a MIDI file.

Sometimes as a strange thought that refuses to leave your head.

SoLuna is built around that journey:

```text
       Feeling
          ↓
        Idea
          ↓
       Prompt
          ↓
      Generation
          ↓
      Exploration
          ↓
       Creation
          ↓
       Something
          ↓
         ✨
       Music
```

The goal isn't simply to generate music.

It's to create a space where **humans and machines can explore musical ideas together.**

> **You bring the feeling.**
> **We help it find a voice.** 🌙
